How it works

Three steps to a completely private conversation

01
Create a room
One tap generates a unique 6-digit code. No sign up, no email, no password. Just a code.
02
Verify the handshake
The joiner receives a verification code to read back to you. You confirm it matches before anyone enters. No uninvited guests.
03
Chat and disappear
Your conversation exists only while you're both present. When either person leaves, it's gone — from the app and from our servers.
Why VantaChat

Built around privacy,
not around your data

Zero accounts
No sign up, no email, no phone number. Nothing that identifies you before, during, or after a chat.
No message logs
Messages aren't stored anywhere permanently. The moment you leave, the conversation is deleted from our database.
Verified entry
The challenge-response handshake means only the person you give the code to can join. No link forwarding, no gate crashing.
Ephemeral by design
Conversations exist only in the moment. There's no history to screenshot, subpoena, or leak later.
Works on any device
No app download required. Open a browser, go to the link, start chatting. Works on phone, tablet, or desktop.
Just two people
One creator. One joiner. A private channel between exactly two people — nobody else can listen in.
